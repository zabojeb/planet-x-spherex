ADR-0023: Add 2MASS, AllWISE and NEOWISE epochs on the sequence's own grid

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-14

Context

SPHEREx covers 2025–2026, so even the fastest test targets move only 1–2 SPHEREx pixels in its data. The analog review (docs/research/analogs.md, gap 1) ranks a longer baseline first: WiseView's value comes from a decade of WISE epochs. IRSA lists twomass_allsky, wise_allwise and neowiser in its CAOM TAP tables; the AllWISE Atlas tiles (4095² float32, 67 MB) and NEOWISE-R frames (1016², ~650 science frames per position over 2014–2024) are uncompressed in the public nasa-irsa-wise bucket (us-west-2); 2MASS scan images are 1.5 MB gzip files on IRSA's file server. The IRSA cutout service was overloaded (ADR-0002).

Decision

  • Discovery: one CAOM TAP query per survey (spherex_core/archival/sources.py); 2MASS and AllWISE with INTERSECTS(p.poly, CIRCLE(...)) (IRSA accepts INTERSECTS only with the column first), NEOWISE with CONTAINS(POINT, p.poly). Cached for a week.
  • Pixels: AllWISE and NEOWISE through our range reader from S3 (only the rows covering the field); 2MASS downloaded whole and decompressed (archival/reader.py).
  • Calibration: DN → MJy/sr with the header MAGZP and Vega zero points (2MASS: Cohen et al. 2003; WISE: Jarrett et al. 2011), so archival frames share units with the SPHEREx co-adds.
  • Resampling: background-subtract, Gaussian-smooth to the SPHEREx scale (σ = 0.35 × pixel-scale ratio, NaN-aware), bilinear resample onto the sequence's grid (same TAN projection, centre and 6.15″ pixels), optionally a wider concentric grid (fov_arcmin) for targets that left the field long ago.
  • Epochs: 2MASS images within 3 days are mosaicked into one epoch per band; all AllWISE tiles touching the field are mosaicked into one epoch; NEOWISE single exposures are median-combined per visit (new visit after 20 days or once a visit spans 30 days; at least 3 frames).
  • Storage and API: a separate result under <cache>/archival/<sequence id>/<n>/ (archival/store.py); GET /api/sequences/{id}/archival (202 while building, first call starts it) and .../archival.bin. Each frame records the target's predicted pixel and whether it is inside the field.

Consequences

  • Barnard's Star on a 12′ grid: 47 frames from 2000 to 2024 (2MASS, AllWISE, 20 NEOWISE visits × W1/W2); the light-weighted centroid sits 0.1–2.5″ from the proper-motion prediction in every frame; 134 s and 1.1 GB for the first build. ε Indi B at 16′: 50 frames, 0.2–1.4″ offsets, 130 s.
  • Builds are seconds-to-minutes and read up to ~1.8 GB for 20′ fields, so they are background tasks (ADR-0025) and cached; the bucket is in us-west-2, ~70 ms from the US-East server.
  • Not for solar-system objects (404). 2MASS coverage can be partial at wide fields; coverage reports it.

Alternatives considered

  • unWISE time-domain co-adds: not in the IRSA bucket (only unwise/neo7/catalogs/); IRSA's wise_unwise collection holds full-depth co-adds without time bounds, which smear movers.
  • IRSA IBE cutouts: simpler, but the service was returning 503s and ~95 s per cutout during the hackathon.
  • Separate native-resolution viewer for archival images: sharper, but the client could not blink them against SPHEREx without a second grid; one grid keeps the movie consistent.
