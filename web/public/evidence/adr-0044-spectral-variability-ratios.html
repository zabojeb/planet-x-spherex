ADR-0044: Spectral variability as ratio spectra between epochs, ring points included

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-14

Context

RW Aur and R Dra are saturated: their photometry is ring flux, relative within a detector (ADR-0020), so no absolute spectrum exists, yet their variability is the point of the test. Ratios between epochs are scale-free.

Decision

spectral/variability.py bins every epoch on the grid (calibrated and ring points separately), divides each epoch by a reference epoch (default: the one with most bins) bin by bin with a 3 % systematic per bin, drops bins with S/N < 3 in either epoch, and reports per-bin ratios with z-scores, per-detector weighted ratios, χ²/dof against "constant" and the detectors that changed by ≥ 3σ.

Consequences

RW Aur's 4–5 µm fade and short-wavelength brightening come out of the same endpoint as a constant star's flat ratios. Blends whose relative position changes between epochs (WISE 0855 and its neighbour) can show spurious ratios; the S/N cut removes the worst cases but the result must be read with the summary's caveats.

Alternatives considered

  • Per-epoch absolute spectra only: impossible for saturated stars.
