ADR-0043: Flag infrared excess and composite spectra from a short-wavelength fit

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-11-14

Context

WISE 0855 sits 8″ from a G=16.3 star in 2025–26, so its SPHEREx photometry is a blend: a normal star below 2.5 µm and the cold Y dwarf at 4–5 µm. Discs around young stars and cold companions give the same signature. A single label from templates (which stop at 2.5 µm) would call it a K star and miss the interesting part.

Decision

A blackbody + extinction fit restricted to λ ≤ 2.45 µm is extrapolated to 3–3.8 and 3.8–5 µm; the observed / predicted ratio and its significance (with 9 % extrapolation uncertainty) are reported as ir_excess. The summary labels a source composite when templates prefer a normal star but the spectrum shows methane at 3.3 µm and a > 1.5×, > 5σ excess at 3.8–5 µm; yso_candidate when a star shows the excess and α > −1.6; brown_dwarf when an L/T standard wins or methane is strong.

Consequences

WISE 0855's sequence reads "composite: + cold methane-rich source" instead of a wrong single type. Saturated stars (ring photometry only) have no calibrated spectrum and get no classification.

Alternatives considered

  • Two-component template fits (star + brown dwarf): no brown-dwarf templates beyond 2.5 µm to make it work.
